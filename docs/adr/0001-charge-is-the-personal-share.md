# A charge is the personal share, not the monthly share

The dashboard compares subscriptions by monthly share, because a yearly plan and a monthly plan have to sit on one axis. A date on the calendar is a different question: the money that leaves on that day is the personal share of the cycle price. A €120 yearly plan shared by two people is €10 a month and a €60 charge. Showing €10 beside the due date would train people to ignore the number. The horizon and any later reminder use the charge. Monthly share stays the language of totals, charts, and work time.
