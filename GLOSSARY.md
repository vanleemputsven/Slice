# Slice

Slice is the record of what a person pays again and again, and what that costs in work time.

## Language

**Subscription**:
A recurring service the person pays for, with one cycle price and one next charge date.
_Avoid_: Bill, expense, plan

**Cycle price**:
The amount billed each time a subscription charges.
_Avoid_: Monthly price, total

**Personal share**:
The person's portion of a cycle price. It is the whole cycle price when the subscription is not shared.
_Avoid_: Split, my cut

**Monthly share**:
The personal share spread evenly over a month, so a yearly subscription can be compared with a monthly one.
_Avoid_: Burn, average

**Charge**:
One debit of the personal share, on the date that subscription next bills.
_Avoid_: Payment, renewal

**Renewal horizon**:
The charges dated today through the next 14 days.
_Avoid_: Upcoming, this week

**Reminder lead**:
The span of days before a charge, including the due date, in which Slice marks that charge. The usual span is three days.
_Avoid_: Notification, alert

**Work time**:
The monthly share expressed as hours and workdays at the person's own rate.
_Avoid_: Burn rate
